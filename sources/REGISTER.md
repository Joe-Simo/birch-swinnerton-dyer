## Load-bearing research sources

**S4 — Ashay A. Burungale and Ye Tian, “A rank zero p-converse to a theorem of Gross–Zagier, Kolyvagin and Rubin.”**
Inspected text: arXiv:2506.03465v2, 11 October 2025, seven pages.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03465
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.03465
Published record: Annals of Mathematics 203 (2026), no.1, pp.1–13.
https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2026/203-1/p01
Theorem 1.1 (preprint p.1): E/K has CM by an order in the imaginary quadratic field K; for any prime p, zero p-infinity Selmer corank implies L(E/K,1) is nonzero. The rational CM consequence is stated by the authors. Theorem 2.6 is a rational characteristic-ideal identity for CM forms, not an integral claim. Theorem 3.1/proof (pp.5–6) passes from zero Bloch–Kato Selmer space to vanishing of localized H^2 and a nonvanishing specialized zeta class, then explicit reciprocity. This proof segment and hypotheses were read. The Kato and Johnson–Leung–Kings dependencies were not independently rebuilt. Publication metadata and inspected preprint pagination are distinguished; no line-by-line published-version comparison was made.

**S5 — Benjamin Howard, “Derived p-adic heights and p-adic L-functions.”**
Inspected arXiv:1202.6343v1, 28 February 2012, 20 pages; published American Journal of Mathematics 126 (2004), no.6, 1315–1340, DOI 10.1353/ajm.2004.0045.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.6343
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.6343
Inspected Section 4 setup (preprint p.15), Theorem 4.2 (p.16), Corollary 4.3 (p.17), including screenshots. Assumptions include p>2, F unramified at p, good ordinary reduction above p, ramification of primes above p and finite decomposition of bad primes in the chosen Z_p-extension. Derived-height kernels measure higher augmentation blocks; even-length block multiplicities are even. Ordinary height nondegeneracy excludes higher blocks and the free part. Applicable as motivation and a precisely conditional bridge, not as an unconditional all-curves theorem. Historical conjecture comments were not promoted to a complete current status survey.
