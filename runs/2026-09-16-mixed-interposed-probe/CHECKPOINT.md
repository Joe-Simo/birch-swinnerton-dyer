## H25: mixed interposed shears reopen the local target, but no short synchronized candidate survives

Continuation of H24. H22 and H24 close the pure horizontal and pure vertical two-parameter families. H25 begins the genuinely mixed architecture in which, after the first Phi, both elementary shears

    H(P,Q)=(P-2Q,Q),
    V(P,Q)=(P,Q-2P)

and their inverses may be used before the final Phi. This checkpoint is deliberately bounded: it is a route-selection experiment, not an all-word theorem and not a BSD candidate.

### 1. Complete mixed orbit at the target prime

At `p=313`, for either first-stage horizontal class `m=0,1` and either embedding `sqrt(137)=78,235`, the first Phi leads to the same changed quadratic field used in H22-H24. Starting from its lifted pair `(P1,Q1)`, exhaustive closure under

    H, H^{-1}, V, V^{-1}

contains exactly

    27,648 states.

Among those states, applying the final Phi gives

    592 regular states with F_+(x)=0 mod313,
    1,128 singular/ambiguous final-Phi states.

The counts agree for `m=0` and `m=1`. Thus the pure-family obstructions do not extend merely from the existence of H22/H24: mixed interposed words have abundant local 313 reachability.

Shortest regular target-zero words include

    identity,
    H V,
    V H,
    H^{-2},
    V^{-2}.

The identity is the H18 `Phi^2` route; `H^{-2}` includes the H21 architecture after the appropriate initial shear.

### 2. Bounded synchronized-prime test

For each `m=0,1`, breadth-first search gives 59 target-zero states whose shortest representative word has length at most 6. Each such representative was then evaluated at every auxiliary prime below 600 for which `(137/ell)=-1`; there are 52 such primes in the tested set.

Every one of those 118 `(m,word)` representatives has at least one **regular** auxiliary-prime failure: either the final F_+ value is zero at `ell !=313` or its quadratic character differs from `(313/ell)`. No length-at-most-six shortest target-state representative survives all 52 tests.

A longer global word can reduce to the same interposed state modulo 313 while reducing to a different state at an auxiliary prime, so replacing the full synchronized word problem by one shortest representative per 313-state would be invalid.

### 3. Failed product-search attempt and next obligation

A direct breadth-first product-state search for simultaneous acceptance at `313` and `13` was attempted, but the computation exceeded the bounded execution window before completing. Its partial state count is discarded and supplies no theorem or exclusion.

The next mathematically correct object is the synchronized congruence action of the subgroup generated by H and V on the product of the relevant finite local orbits. The next implementation should exploit the matrix action of the elementary shears (rather than storing full elliptic coordinates at every word) and either:

1. produce a short word satisfying the target condition at 313 and several independent auxiliary characters, followed by exact rational reconstruction; or
2. prove a finite product-orbit obstruction for the entire mixed interposed group.

No inference about arbitrary mixed words, analytic order, all elliptic curves or general BSD is made here.
