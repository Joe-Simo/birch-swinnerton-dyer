## [CFS] Minimal models and reduction

John E. Cremona, Tom Fisher and Michael Stoll, *Minimisation and reduction of 2-, 3- and 4-coverings of elliptic curves*, arXiv:0908.1741v1, 2009-08-12, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.1741v1 . Read Theorem 1.1 (physical pages 2–3), Section 6, Theorem 6.1, Corollary 6.3 and Definition 6.4 (physical pages 40–41), and the surrounding reduction discussion. The class-number-one and local-solubility hypotheses are retained. Minimal discriminant and a unimodular coordinate reduction do not supply the missing small global point. The publication is *Algebra & Number Theory* 4 (2010), 763–820, DOI 10.2140/ant.2010.4.763; theorem numbering is pinned to the inspected v1.
