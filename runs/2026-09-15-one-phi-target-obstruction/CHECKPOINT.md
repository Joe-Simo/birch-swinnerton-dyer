## H17: one-Phi target-prime obstruction for 313

Continuation of H16 on the private BSD branch `research/h2-endpoint-20260914`. This checkpoint concerns only the 137-seeded quartic transport architecture. It does not prove prescribed-prime reachability, exact analytic order for a new family, a universal rank theorem, or BSD.

### 1. Exact target and family

Use the H15/H16 seed on the two copies of `E0: v^2=u^3-u`:

    P=((197+17*sqrt(137))/2, 1393+119*sqrt(137)),
    Q=((-1173+47*sqrt(137))/2738,
       (-27377+329*sqrt(137))/50653).

The quartic coordinate maps are

    t=(u+e)/v,     r=(2u-e)t^2-1,

with `e=+1` for the first factor and `e=-1` for the second. Let

    H(P,Q)=(P-2Q,Q),
    h(P,Q)=(P+2Q,Q),
    V(P,Q)=(P,Q-2P),
    v(P,Q)=(P,Q+2P).

Every map commutes with the translated deck involution and descends to the quotient surface. Separate odd multiplication on either factor also descends. After such a pre-Phi word, apply the authenticated osculating map `Phi`; its x-coordinate is the cancelled H14 formula, with `x'=-1/x^3` on the already-proved removable boundary `C=0`.

For a rational output to have squareclass equal to an odd prime `p`, `F_+(x')=x'^4+6x'^2+1` must have odd p-adic valuation. In particular its reduction modulo p must vanish. Therefore an exhaustive residue calculation with every Phi chart regular can rigorously exclude that target prime for the specified family.

### 2. The one-parameter mixed family H^m v Phi misses both targets

First examine

    P_m=P-2mQ,
    Q_m=Q+2P_m=2P+(1-4m)Q,

### 3. Stronger closure theorem at 313

The previous two-row calculation leaves open other linear combinations. Enlarge the pre-Phi family to the semigroup generated by

    H, h, V, v,

and **arbitrary separate odd multiplication on either E0 factor**. At a fixed target prime, odd multiplication need only be taken modulo the exact local orders, so the orbit closure is finite and exhaustive.

For `p=313`, under each of the two embeddings `sqrt(137)=78,235`, the closure contains exactly

    576 states.

Every one of those 576 states lies in a valid quartic/Phi chart: there are zero ambiguous states. After applying one `Phi` to every state, the number satisfying

    F_+(Phi_x)=0 mod313

is exactly zero.

Therefore:

> No word formed from horizontal/vertical ±2 shears and arbitrary separate odd multiplication on the two E0 factors, followed by one Phi, can produce rational output squareclass 313 from the 137 seed.

This includes arbitrary repetitions of the shears and all local odd multipliers, hence in particular separate tripling. The conclusion is only for **one final Phi**. It does not cover words containing an earlier Phi, a changed nonlinear fiber map, or an unlisted surface transformation.

### 4. Contrast with 257

The identical exhaustive calculation at `p=257`, under both embeddings `sqrt(137)=44,213`, gives

    1024 states,
    96 states with F_+(Phi_x)=0 mod257,
    16 states whose quartic/Phi chart is ambiguous at this precision.

Thus the corresponding one-Phi local obstruction is false for 257: the enlarged semigroup contains many locally 257-divisible outputs. This is consistent with H15, where 257 appeared as a factor of a composite output. Local p-divisibility still does not show that the global squarefree kernel equals p; unwanted cofactors remain the global obstruction.

### 6. Consequence and next mathematical obligation

The 313 obstruction is now structural for a substantially enlarged **one-Phi** transport class: linear mixing and every separate odd multiplier before the final nonlinear map are insufficient. Any continuation toward 313 within this architecture must introduce a genuinely nonlinear operation before the final Phi—for example an earlier Phi or a changed fiber map.

For 257 the situation is the opposite: local divisibility is available, so the informative next problem is global cofactor control rather than another local existence test. The best next split is therefore (i) search the smallest rigorously defined two-Phi class for a 313-adic escape, while (ii) analyze the squareclass-transfer/cofactor structure of one of the 96 locally 257-divisible states. Neither direction is a complete BSD candidate.
