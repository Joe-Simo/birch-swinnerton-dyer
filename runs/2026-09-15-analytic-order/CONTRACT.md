# H7 pre-execution contract

Status: no complete BSD candidate.
Target: independently certify the complex analytic order for E_p:y^2=x^3-p^2x, starting with p=41 (whose rational rank two has an independent Kummer/descent certificate).
Follow-up population, only after the first certificate works: the seven exact-zero primes from H4, p=41,137,257,313,353,457,761; no wider scan. This tests whether a second or higher even zero occurs in this fixed list, not a universal rank assertion.

Use exact point-count/Euler-factor coefficients, exact central vanishing from Tunnell counts, and the sign +1 functional equation, with conductor 32p^2 independently sourced. Prove the Mellin integral derivative formula and explicit error bounds for both Fourier and integration tails. Nonzero numerical approximations are discovery only. Accept nonvanishing only if a mathematically justified enclosure excludes zero. A failure of an enclosure proves nothing about analytic order.

Attempt a certified interval-arithmetic library; no paid services or external research contact. Computation bounds, quadrature subdivisions, and error formulas will be recorded with the actual accepted outputs. Any tuning after exploratory output will be disclosed, not called preregistered.

The height branch is not assumed solved: neither persistent descent nor a rank-two Selmer result is silently replaced by rational points. An analytic rank-two certificate alone will not certify algebraic rank two for the other six primes.
